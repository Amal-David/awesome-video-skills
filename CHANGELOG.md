# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-01

Initial public release: 193 pinned upstream skill paths across 41 repositories; 29 model/backend/specialized workflow surfaces; six render engines; two controller profiles; 24 decision workflows; and 11 original installable skills.

Added offline capability routing, six-calendar-month recency preference, provider-specific adapters, per-operation limits, lexical retrieval, JSONL indexing, schemas, source provenance and self-contained host installation. Included version-specific LTX multishot, EXR, in/outpainting, restoration, dubbing and Foley decisions rather than inheriting every capability across versions.

The initial validation suite covers 76 offline routing/packaging cases. All 193 pinned paths were checked against their recorded commit trees. Paid generation, upstream dependency installation and perceptual output benchmarks were not run.
