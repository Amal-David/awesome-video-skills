## Corrections that change the choice of workflow

The refreshed [HyperFrames talking-head-recut](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/86521b94f592bcf0ca86aba63068893cf658af16/skills/talking-head-recut/SKILL.md) preserves the original take and adds graphic cards. It is not a transcript-cutting tool. Its audio skill mixes already placed media; media sourcing/generation belongs elsewhere. The [audio reference](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/86521b94f592bcf0ca86aba63068893cf658af16/docs/reference/audio-effects.mdx) distinguishes clip-local envelopes, composition-time group automation and the root master effect bus.

The refreshed [Remotion interactivity skill](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills/blob/7ba4dcf8ebe1626b00baa41e1f2478113719b60b/skills/remotion-interactivity/SKILL.md) links independently editable Studio elements to their authored JSX nodes. The motion guide now makes that deliverable decision explicit. See [workflow recipes](../docs/workflow-recipes.md) for these distinctions in context.

Runway's current contracts also prevent useful but incorrect shortcuts: first-image generation is not first/last-frame control, outpainting is not temporal extension, and an opaque ProRes 4444 plane is not usable transparency. Professional formats and native HDR support stay tied to the exact surface. See [the Runway guide](../docs/models/runway.md).

## Routing fixes from the audit

- Preserve global runtime, media privacy, host, controller and tool constraints across both hybrid stages.
- Retain inferred capabilities in the saved normalized request so replaying it does not weaken the brief.
- Require matching input counts when reference durations are supplied, and validate source-audio duration against the resolved task.
- Keep operation guidance distinct from reference-based creation: editing keyframes do not imply a general reference-to-video capability.
- Enforce discrete allowed durations without inventing frame-rate support.
- Resolve specialized inpainting, outpainting, restoration, EXR/HDR, dubbing, Foley and enhancement queries before the generic video rule.
- Reject blank selectors and handle large valid JSON integers without a float-conversion overflow.

The builder now derives the manifest and public counts from the canonical data and propagates the snapshot date into all generated indexes and skill bundles. Counts and date changes no longer require editing multiple hardcoded output strings.

## Evaluation guidance

The new [evaluation protocol](../docs/evaluation.md) uses VideoWeaver's distinction between process and output evaluation, and [VISTA's](https://g-vista.github.io/) candidate comparison and focused critique as research inputs. The proposed practice is to freeze the brief, preserve a baseline, log every attempt, diagnose the failing stage and check the revision on additional cases. This is original operational guidance; these papers' benchmark results were not reproduced here.

## Verification boundary

The repository checks validate catalog consistency, constraint behavior, standalone installation and generated-file synchronization. The [pin report](pin-verification.json) records immutable path/blob verification. Neither proves account access, API availability, upstream dependency safety or rendered quality. No paid video jobs or upstream renderer executions were performed during this update.
